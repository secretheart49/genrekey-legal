1. Scope

This policy covers the SongKey app (formerly GenreKey) for Android (package com.songkeyfinder.app) from version 1.2.0, and the SongKey chart server it uses for Trending. SongKey needs no account.

Version 1.0 (named GenreKey): this earlier version sends the audio you choose to analyse, a live sample or a file you pick, to SongKey's analysis server. The server works out the result and does not keep the audio. Updating the app keeps all analysis on your phone.

2. Your Audio Stays on Your Phone

SongKey works out a song's genre, mood, tempo (BPM), key, time signature and energy entirely on your phone. It never uploads your recordings, the files you choose, their names or their tags.

  • Live listening: the microphone is used only while you hold or tap the listen button, and listening stops if you leave the app. Recordings are never saved.
  • Files you choose: SongKey reads only the file you pick and never changes it.

3. What Stays on Your Phone

Your recent results stay on your phone so you can open them again. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

4. Trending Charts

Trending is the only feature that uses the internet. When you open it, the app asks SongKey's chart server for the charts of the country you choose. Like every internet request, this shows your IP address; the chart server does not keep it and never sees your scans.

Chart artwork loads from the chart provider, which sees your IP address like any website you visit; its own privacy policy applies to those requests.

5. Crash Reports

When SongKey crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to a crash-reporting service that works for us, so we can find and fix the problem. A report holds the error and where in the app it happened, the app version, your phone model, system version and free memory and storage, and a random ID for this installation. It never includes your audio, recordings, file names or results. The service keeps reports for 90 days, protects them under its data processing terms and uses them only to provide this service to us. You can turn crash reports off at any time in the app's menu.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone: only for live listening, as described above.
  • Files: the system file picker; SongKey sees only the file you pick.
  • Internet: Trending charts and artwork, and crash reports.

SongKey sends no notifications and does not run in the background.

7. Sharing

SongKey has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not sell or share personal data. The only services involved are SongKey's chart server, the chart provider for artwork and the crash-reporting service. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

8. Your Choices

  • SongKey works without an internet connection; only Trending needs one.
  • You can deny or remove microphone access in your phone's settings and still analyse files.
  • You can turn crash reports on or off in the app's menu.
  • You can remove everything SongKey stores by clearing its app data or uninstalling it.
  • You can email us to ask about your data or to have anything held about you deleted.

9. Children

SongKey is not designed for children under 13. Only record or choose audio you have the right to use.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when SongKey changes and show the date at the top. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: articlespinnerai@gmail.com.
